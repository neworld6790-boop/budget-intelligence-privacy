Delete your Budget Intelligence account

App: Budget Intelligence · Developer: Akil Ramcharitar

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Budget Intelligence and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose "Delete my account and data".
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

What is deleted

  • All your budgets and spending entries stored in the cloud, any Smart mode answer reports you sent, and your user record.
  • All data stored on your device: savings goals, rewards, onboarding answers, profile, settings, remembered email and any custom avatar photo.
  • Your sign-in account.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Google may take some time to fully remove deleted data from its backups.

If you cannot open the app

Email neworld6790@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Budget Intelligence account". We will delete your account and cloud data and reply within 30 days.
