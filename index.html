Budget Intelligence Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Introduction

Budget Intelligence is a budgeting app provided by Akil Ramcharitar. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By using Budget Intelligence you agree to this policy.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app:

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save your budgets and spending and sync them across your devices.
  • To show your goals, rewards and reminders on your device.
  • To power the optional AI features described below.
  • To protect the app and your data from abuse and tampering.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

AI features (Smart Mode)

Smart Mode is optional, and you can switch it off in the app. When it is on, tips, check-ups, coaching, chat and "turn this sentence into an entry" send text to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic. Each request includes:

  • A summary of your current budget: currency symbol, income and expense line names and amounts, totals, remaining cushion, days left, recent daily spending totals, and goal names, targets and progress.
  • The text you type, such as a chat question or a sentence like "spent $12 on coffee".

We do not send receipt photos, your email address or your sign-in details to the AI service. Chat history is kept in memory during your session and is not saved on your device.

Google processes these requests to generate the response, under Google's terms for Firebase and the Gemini API. On Google's free tier, content sent to the AI service may be used to improve Google's products. If you do not want this, switch Smart Mode off. AI responses are general information, not financial advice.

Security

  • In transit: All communication with Firebase and the AI service uses HTTPS (TLS). The app blocks unencrypted connections.
  • In the cloud: Google encrypts your account and budget data at rest. Access rules let each signed-in user read and write only their own data. This data is not end-to-end encrypted, because the service needs to read it to sync your budgets.
  • On your device: Savings goals and onboarding answers are encrypted with an AES-256-GCM key held in the Android Keystore. Other app data sits in the app's private storage and is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We use these providers to run the app, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, AI Logic (Gemini) and App Check.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple sign-in: only if you choose them to sign in.
  • Google Play Integrity: checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the app and may send device and app integrity signals to Google.

Receipt scanning uses on-device text recognition, so the photo and recognized text never leave your phone. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Data retention and deleting your account

We keep your account and budget data until you delete your account. To delete it, go to Settings and choose "Delete my account and data", then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes:

  1. All your budgets and spending in Firestore, any Smart mode answer reports you sent, and your user record.
  2. All data on your device: goals, rewards, onboarding answers, profile, settings, remembered email and any custom avatar photo.
  3. Your Firebase Authentication account. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Reports you send about a Smart mode answer, including the question and answer you report, are stored so we can review them, and are deleted together with your account.

Deletion cannot be undone. Logging out alone does not delete anything. Uninstalling the app removes the data on your device but not your cloud data.

If you cannot open the app, email neworld6790@gmail.com from your account email address and ask us to delete your account. We will respond within 30 days. Google may take some time to fully remove deleted data from its backups. See also the account deletion page.

Your choices and rights

  • Switch Smart Mode off to stop budget summaries being sent to the AI service.
  • Turn off reminders in the app or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Withdraw camera access in your phone's settings. The app uses your camera app only when you scan a receipt.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing neworld6790@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, such as the EU, UK or California, you may have additional legal rights over your data. Contact us and we will help you use them.

Children's privacy

Budget Intelligence is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
